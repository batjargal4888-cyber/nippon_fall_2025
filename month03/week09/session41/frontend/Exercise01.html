My Exercise Plan

Welcome to My Exercise Plan!

Are you ready to get started on your fitness journey? This simple exercise plan will help you build a foundation for a healthier and more active lifestyle.

Day 1: Walking

Start with a 15-minute brisk walk around your neighborhood or a nearby park. Focus on maintaining good posture and breathing rhythmically.

Day 2: Stretching

Perform a 10-minute stretching routine to improve flexibility. Include stretches for your neck, shoulders, arms, legs, and back.

Day 3: Squats

Do 3 sets of 10 squats. Squats are great for building lower body strength. Keep your back straight, and go as low as you comfortably can.

Day 4: Rest

Rest is essential for muscle recovery. Take a break today and focus on staying hydrated.

Day 5: Push-ups

Perform 3 sets of 5 push-ups. If regular push-ups are too challenging, start with knee push-ups and work your way up.

Day 6: Jumping Jacks

Do 3 sets of 15 jumping jacks. This is a great way to improve cardiovascular fitness and burn calories.

Day 7: Rest

Another day of rest to allow your body to recover. Focus on healthy eating and staying active by walking or stretching.

Remember:

Stay hydrated throughout your workouts.

Listen to your body; don't push too hard, especially if you're just starting out.

Consistency is key. Try to stick to your exercise plan and gradually increase intensity as you become more comfortable.

Consult with a healthcare professional before beginning any new exercise program, especially if you have any underlying health conditions.
